# Domain Seminars

One short slide deck per domain seminar. Each seminar gives the healthcare administration context for that week's lab: the business setting, the policy behind it, and how analytics is used to make decisions.

Each deck has:

- a title slide
- five content slides of brief bullets, each with **speaker notes** (with sources for figures) to review and edit:
  1. **Basics:** the topic's core concepts and terminology
  2. **Where it sits:** industry presence and where it shows up in policy
  3. **Reform goals:** current reform goals and future considerations
  4. **How analytics is deployed** in the domain
  5. **Analytics in decisions:** how analytics drives decision making, covering the business question students take on in the matching lab
