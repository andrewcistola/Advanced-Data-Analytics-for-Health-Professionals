# Special Lectures

Three special lectures that pull the course together. Each is adapted from the instructor's own talk and frames the human and professional side of analytics work that the weekly seminars and labs build toward.

| # | Seminar | Theme |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [All Data Problems Are Human Problems](special01_all_data_problems_are_human_problems.pptx) | Data quality, governance, QA and deliverables fail for human reasons, so the fixes are human: agreement, documentation, shared QA and asking people what they actually use |
| 2 | [Liability over Capability](special02_liability_over_capability.pptx) | As AI makes analytic capability cheap and widespread, the scarce skill is standing behind the result: technical fluency to review AI's work, validation designed for the user, and clear accountability |
| 3 | [Virtues for the Data Analyst](special03_virtues_for_the_data_analyst.pptx) | As AI takes over routine analysis, an analyst's value comes from character as much as skill: courage, wisdom, justice and restraint, adapted from the four cardinal virtues |

Each deck has a title slide, six content slides and the same closing **Discussion** slide used in the domain and management seminars, with **speaker notes** that follow the original talk.

The decks use the same plain format as the other seminar decks (black background, white Arial text, **Title Slide** and **Title and Content** layouts, all text in placeholders). See the [technical seminars README](../technical-seminars/README.md) for how to apply an organization's slide master.
